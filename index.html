Hi, welcome to my portfolio. I have been building it since my undergraduate years, and I recently completed my post-graduation in Physics from the University of Dhaka. During the COVID lockdown, I developed self-taught programming skills with a strong interest in simulation and computational physics. Visualization plays a key role in this field—since many physics problems involve complex boundary conditions where direct experiments are costly or impractical, simulations are essential for building reliable models and avoiding futile trials. My interests grew from this realization, leading me to create small projects focused on visualizing simple physics models and developing innovative, practical tools. Even my thesis research is rooted in this interest, as it centers on DFT simulations (To visit my DFT blog: HPii_DFT_World). Here, I’m showcasing only my side projects—click on the images to learn more.
